Club Grant Program

Grants range from $1,000 to $25,000 and are funded by Amateur Radio Digital Communications.

Applicants must be an ARRL-affiliated club in good standing.

2024 Recipients

  • Kansas State University Amateur Radio Club, KS — $18,000
  • Missouri S&T Amateur Radio Club, MO — $12,500
  • Oklahoma State University ARC, OK — $9,000
  • Baylor University WA5BU, TX — $7,400
  • City Tech Amateur Radio Club, NY — $5,000
