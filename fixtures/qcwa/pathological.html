QCWA Memorial Scholarship Fund

Each scholarship is $3,000.

The applicant must be sponsored by an active QCWA member in good standing.

Awards go to licensed amateur radio operators enrolled in accredited colleges.

Requests are accepted from October 31 and must reach ARRL before the first week of January.

Applications are submitted through the ARRL Foundation scholarship application, not to QCWA.

Historical funds were previously administered at the FAR website.
